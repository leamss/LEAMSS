DAY-TO-DAY

Typical tasks performed

  1. 1 Using test equipment to locate electrical and electronic malfunctions
  2. 2 Dismantling and removing electrical and electronic assemblies and components
  3. 3 Installing electrical equipment and electronic components in motor vehicles
  4. 4 Connecting power-operated vehicle equipment and accessories to power supply
  5. 5 Adjusting engine control systems and timing
  6. 6 Testing and replacing defective alternators, generators, voltage regulators and starter motors
  7. 7 Repairing and replacing faulty ignition and electrical wiring
  8. 8 Replacing defective parts such as fuses, lamps and switches
