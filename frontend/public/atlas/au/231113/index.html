DAY-TO-DAY

Typical tasks performed

  1. 1 Preparing and submitting flight plans giving consideration to factors such as weather conditions and aircraft performance
  2. 2 Flying aircraft in accordance with established air traffic control and aircraft operating procedures
  3. 3 Providing flight information for flight crews and air traffic services staff
  4. 4 Controlling aircraft movements, and directing aircraft taxiing, take-offs and landings by radio
  5. 5 Providing pre-flight briefings and aeronautical information services
  6. 6 Completing cockpit preparations and external inspections to determine that aircraft are acceptable for flight
  7. 7 Monitoring aircraft performance and reporting on mechanical condition
  8. 8 Giving in-flight instruction, supervising solo flights, accompanying students on training flights and demonstrating techniques for controlling aircraft
